# Pearlz Home Systems — Customer Service Handbook
Document ID: HB-CS-2025-V1
Internal Operations Guidelines for Support Staff

## 1. Operating Hours and Support Channels
Support desk operations run six days a week across two shifts:
- Shift 1: 08:00 AM to 04:00 PM (PKT)
- Shift 2: 04:00 PM to 12:00 AM Midnight (PKT)
- Official Channels: Support Portal (support.pearlzhome.example), Email (support@pearlzhome.example), WhatsApp Desk (+92-42-111-PEARLZ).

## 2. Ticket Priority Definitions and Response SLAs
- **Priority 1 (Critical)**: Major appliance failure involving active water leakage (e.g. Error E-05), electrical short, or water supply disruption. First response within 30 minutes; technician dispatch within 4 hours.
- **Priority 2 (High)**: Purifier stopped dispensing water, alert code E-03/E-04, or pump malfunction. First response within 2 hours; technician resolution within 24 hours.
- **Priority 3 (Medium)**: Order status queries, delivery inquiries, or scheduled filter replacement requests. First response within 4 hours.
- **Priority 4 (Low)**: General pre-sale inquiries, accessory pricing, or user manual requests. First response within 8 hours.

## 3. Tone of Voice and Communication Rules
- Maintain an empathetic, professional, transparent, and direct tone.
- Avoid internal abbreviations (e.g., do not say 'DOA RMA issued', say 'Your complimentary replacement unit has been approved').
- Never speculate or promise compensation, refunds, or technical exceptions that exceed documented policies.

## 4. Escalation Matrix and Financial Compensation Limits
- Tier 1 Support Representative: Authorized to issue goodwill vouchers up to PKR 500 for delivery delays.
- Tier 2 Shift Supervisor: Authorized to approve restocking fee waivers or delivery compensation up to PKR 2,000.
- Customer Operations Head: Sole authority to approve out-of-policy cash refunds or goodwill machine replacements.
