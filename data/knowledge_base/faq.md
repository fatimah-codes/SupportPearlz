## Section 1: Ordering and Payment
### Q01: How can I place an order for a new purifier?
You can order directly through our official website, via authorized Pearlz retail dealers, or by sending a message to our official WhatsApp sales desk at +92-42-111-PEARLZ.

### Q02: What payment options do you support?
We accept Visa and Mastercard credit/debit cards, 1Link online inter-bank funds transfer (IBFT), and Cash on Delivery (COD) for orders up to PKR 50,000.

### Q03: Can I pay through monthly instalments or EMI plans?
We currently do not offer direct installment plans or bank EMI financing. All orders must be settled in full at time of order or upon delivery.

## Section 2: Delivery and Shipping
### Q04: How long will it take to get my purifier delivered to Karachi or Lahore?
For metro cities including Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, and Rawalpindi, standard delivery takes 1 to 2 working days. Orders placed before 2:00 PM are dispatched on the same day.

### Q05: Can I get next-day delivery to Skardu or Gilgit?
No. Next-day express delivery is exclusively offered in Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad. Skardu, Gilgit, and other remote mountain areas fall under Tier 3 shipping with a standard delivery timeline of 5 to 7 working days.

### Q06: Is shipping free on replacement filters?
Standard shipping is free on orders totaling PKR 5,000 or more. If you order a single replacement pre-filter cartridge priced at PKR 1,550, a standard shipping fee of PKR 250 applies unless combined with other items.

## Section 3: Warranty and Guarantee
### Q07: How long is the warranty on the AquaPearl 500 Pro?
The AquaPearl 500 Pro comes with a 24-month standard warranty from the verifiable invoice date, covering the body, booster pump, internal electronics, UV lamp, and digital display.

### Q08: Are filter cartridges covered under the 24-month warranty?
No. Filter cartridges (sediment, activated carbon, RO membrane, mineralizer) are consumable items and are excluded from the standard 24-month warranty. They are only covered against manufacturing defects reported within 48 hours of delivery.

### Q09: If a local plumber opens the machine to fix a wire, is my warranty still valid?
No. Opening, modifying, or servicing the purifier by any unauthorised third party or local technician immediately voids your remaining warranty. All service must be conducted by certified Pearlz technicians.

### Q10: My unit leaked and caused water damage to my kitchen wooden cabinets. Will Pearlz pay for cabinet repair?
No. Under §3.2 of the Warranty Policy and §5.1 of the Service Agreement, warranty coverage applies strictly to the repair or replacement of the purifier unit itself. Consequential damage to external property, including wooden cabinets, flooring, or countertops, is strictly excluded from liability.

### Q11: How do I submit a warranty claim?
To submit a warranty claim, follow the procedure in Service Agreement §4.2: email your stamped invoice copy, machine serial number photograph, and a 15-second video of the issue to claims@pearlzhome.example. Our support team will issue an RMA number within 24 hours.

## Section 4: Installation and Setup
### Q12: What water pressure is required for installing the AquaPearl 500 Pro?
The required inlet water pressure is between 15 PSI and 45 PSI (1.0 bar to 3.1 bar). If your tap pressure is below 15 PSI, you will need a low-pressure booster feed kit. If pressure exceeds 45 PSI, an inline pressure-reducing valve (PRV) must be installed to prevent membrane shock.

### Q13: Does Pearlz provide professional installation and how much does it cost?
Yes. Professional installation by a certified Pearlz technician is available in metro cities for PKR 1,500. It includes wall mounting or under-sink bracket fitting, water line tapping, drain clamp setup, and initial TDS calibration.

### Q14: Can I install the purifier myself without voiding the warranty?
Yes, you may self-install using the provided installation kit. However, drilling holes into the purifier casing or utilizing non-standard third-party brass adapters will void your warranty.

### Q15: Why does water taste weird or chalky after installing a new filter?
When new filters are installed, micro-bubbles of trapped air and food-grade carbon dust particles are released. You must perform the mandatory first-run flushing procedure (see Installation Guide §4): allow the storage tank to fill completely and discard 15 to 20 liters of water before drinking.

## Section 5: Troubleshooting and Audible Alarms
### Q16: My purifier beeps three times and the red light flashes. What does that mean?
Three short beeps accompanied by a flashing red light indicates alert code E-03, signifying that the sediment pre-filter cartridge has become saturated. You should replace the sediment pre-filter cartridge (SKU-FP-01) and reset the alert by pressing and holding the RESET button for 5 seconds.

### Q17: The machine is constantly beeping and will not turn on. Display says E-05.
Error E-05 indicates that the internal leak detection sensor in the bottom chassis has detected water pooling. The unit has triggered an automatic shutoff. Immediately turn off your water inlet tap, unplug the power cord, and contact customer service. Do not attempt to use the machine.

### Q18: Why is my mobile app unable to find my purifier during pairing?
The purifier's WiFi module operates exclusively on 2.4 GHz 802.11b/g/n networks. Ensure your phone is connected to your router's 2.4 GHz SSID (not 5 GHz) and mobile data is temporarily toggled off during setup.

## Section 6: Returns and Cancellations
### Q19: What is the return window if I change my mind?
You may request a return within 14 calendar days from delivery date for unopened, sealed products in original packaging.

### Q20: Can I return the purifier if I opened the box?
If opened, you may return the unit within 7 calendar days only if it has NOT been hooked up to water mains and remains completely dry. A 15% restocking fee will be deducted from your refund, and you will be responsible for return shipping.

### Q21: How long does a credit card refund take to show on my bank statement?
Once the returned unit passes warehouse inspection, credit card refunds are initiated within 3 to 5 business days.
