# Pearlz Home Systems — Privacy and Data Protection Policy
Document ID: POL-PRV-2025-V2
Effective Date: January 1, 2025

## 1. Information Collected at Purchase and Mobile App Setup
Pearlz Home Systems collects necessary personal identification and transaction records when customers place orders or connect smart IoT appliances:
- Full customer name, national identification number (where required for warranty registration), delivery address, contact email, and mobile telephone number.
- Device telemetry data: daily water volume dispensed (in liters), incoming feed water TDS levels, purified water output TDS levels, filter life decay percentages, and WiFi connectivity logs.
- Technical logs: IP address, mobile application version, mobile operating system version, and crash diagnostic telemetry.

## 2. Water-Usage Telemetry and IoT Data Purpose
IoT telemetry transmitted by smart appliances (AquaPearl 500 Pro and AeroPearl Mini) is used exclusively for:
- Monitoring filter life and dispatching automated filter replacement notifications.
- Diagnosing technical performance issues and scheduling predictive maintenance before pump failure.
- Aggregated, anonymized statistical analytics for improving membrane life and hydraulic efficiency.

## 3. Data Retention Periods
- Transaction and billing data: Retained for seven (7) years to comply with statutory fiscal and tax record obligations.
- IoT water consumption and sensor telemetry: Retained for twenty-four (24) months, after which it is permanently purged or anonymized.
- Customer support ticket logs, chat transcripts, and recorded service calls: Retained for twelve (12) months from ticket resolution date.

## 4. Third-Party Data Sharing
Pearlz Home Systems does not monetize, sell, or rent customer personal data. Data is shared strictly with approved functional service partners:
- Third-party courier and logistics partners (TCS, Leopards Courier) for delivery fulfillment.
- Payment gateway processors for PCI-DSS compliant credit card and banking transaction clearance.
- Cloud hosting infrastructure providers (Amazon Web Services, Frankfurt region) under encrypted data processing agreements.

## 5. User Rights: Access, Deletion, and Data Export
In accordance with consumer privacy standards, customers retain complete control over their personal data:
- **Right to Access**: Request a digital copy of all personal and machine telemetry records stored by Pearlz.
- **Right to Deletion**: Request permanent erasure of account profile and telemetry data upon terminating smart app services.
- **Right to Portability**: Download water consumption logs in CSV format directly via the Pearlz Mobile App profile menu.

To exercise any privacy rights, email privacy@pearlzhome.example. Requests are acknowledged within 48 hours and processed within 14 calendar days.

## 6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies
Our web store utilizes essential functional cookies for shopping basket maintenance, session security, and anonymized analytics cookies to optimize user navigation.
