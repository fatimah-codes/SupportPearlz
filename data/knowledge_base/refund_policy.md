# Pearlz Home Systems — Refund and Returns Policy
Document ID: POL-REF-2025-V2
Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Applicable Products: All consumer hardware, filter cartridges, and accessory bundles

## 1. Return Window Overview
Pearlz Home Systems offers a 14-day customer satisfaction return window.
- The 14-day return window commences on the documented date of carrier delivery confirmation.
- Requests submitted after 14 calendar days from delivery cannot be accepted for refund or commercial exchange.

## 2. Condition Requirements for Returns
### 2.1 Unopened Merchandise
Products that are brand new, completely sealed in original manufacturer packaging, with all plastic security seals, instruction manuals, accessories, and promotional extras intact are eligible for a 100% refund of the product purchase price.
- The customer is responsible for return shipping costs to our central logistics hub in Lahore, unless the return is due to an authenticated dispatch error by Pearlz.

### 2.2 Opened Merchandise and Restocking Fee
If the packaging has been opened, the product unboxed, or protective wraps removed, the return is subject to the following strict conditions:
- The return request must be logged within 7 calendar days of delivery (shortened window for opened items).
- The water purifier or air purifier must NOT have been connected to water mains or operated with wet water flow. Any unit through which wet water has flown cannot be reconditioned for hygiene reasons and is strictly ineligible for return.
- If the unit has been unboxed but remains dry and undamaged, a mandatory 15% restocking fee is deducted from the total refund amount to cover factory re-testing, sanitization, and repacking.
- The customer bears all freight and return transit insurance costs.

### 2.3 Non-Refundable Items
The following items are completely non-refundable once opened:
- Individual replacement filter cartridges with torn vacuum foil seals
- Used filter bundles
- Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) subscription fees after the first technician dispatch
- Custom installation accessories (such as drilled granite brackets or extended tubing runs)

## 3. Defective On Arrival (DOA) Procedure
If an appliance fails to power on or exhibits immediate functional manufacturing defect upon first installation within 48 hours of delivery:
- The customer must notify support@pearlzhome.example with photo/video evidence.
- Pearlz will arrange complimentary courier pickup at company expense.
- Following technical bench assessment, Pearlz will dispatch a brand new replacement unit within 3 business days or issue a 100% full refund with zero deductions.

## 4. Refund Processing Timelines by Payment Method
Upon verified receipt and bench inspection of the returned merchandise at our logistics warehouse (inspection takes 48–72 business hours):
- **Credit / Debit Card Purchases**: Refund is credited back to the original card within 3–5 working days (subject to issuing bank clearance cycle).
- **Online Bank Transfer (IBFT)**: Direct bank transfer to the customer’s verified IBAN within 7–10 working days.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD)**: Cash refunds are disbursed solely via crossed cheque or online bank transfer to customer’s bank account within 7–10 working days after banking verification.

## 5. Distinction Between Return, Replacement, and Warranty Repair
1. **Return**: Voluntary return of an unwanted, dry, resaleable item within 14 days for monetary refund (less applicable restocking and shipping fees).
2. **Replacement**: Immediate exchange of a factory-defective unit within the initial 48-hour DOA window.
3. **Warranty Repair**: Remedy for mechanical or electrical failure occurring during the active 24-month warranty period; involves component replacement or factory repair, not cash refund.

## 6. Promotional Bundles Refund Rules
When products are purchased as part of a bundle (e.g., AquaPearl 500 Pro + Free Annual Filter Bundle):
- If the customer returns the purifier but retains the filter bundle, the full retail list price of the retained filter bundle (PKR 8,500) will be subtracted from the purifier refund amount.
- Partial returns of bundle consumables are not permitted.
