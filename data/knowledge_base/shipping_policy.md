# Pearlz Home Systems — Shipping and Delivery Policy
Document ID: POL-SHP-2025-V4
Effective Date: February 1, 2025

## 1. Geographical Delivery Tiers and Timelines
Pearlz Home Systems ships across Pakistan through our courier network (TCS, Leopards, and Call Courier). Delivery timelines are categorized into three distinct geographical zones:

### Tier 1: Metro Regions
- **Locations**: Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, and Rawalpindi.
- **Standard Delivery Time**: 1 to 2 working days.
- **Order Cut-off for Same-Day Dispatch**: Orders confirmed before 2:00 PM (Monday through Saturday) are dispatched on the same calendar day.

### Tier 2: Secondary Cities and Urban Centers
- **Locations**: Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Gujranwala, Sialkot, Hyderabad, Quetta, Abbottabad, Bahawalpur, and Sukkur.
- **Standard Delivery Time**: 3 to 4 working days.
- **Dispatch**: Orders are dispatched within 24 hours of order validation.

### Tier 3: Remote and Mountain Regions
- **Locations**: Skardu, Gilgit, Hunza, Gwadar, Chitral, Turbat, Mirpur (AJK), and Upper Swat.
- **Standard Delivery Time**: 5 to 7 working days, subject to road access and weather conditions.
- **Express Shipping**: Express and Next-Day delivery options are strictly NOT available for remote and mountainous regions.

## 2. Next-Day Express Delivery
- **Availability**: Next-Day priority courier delivery is exclusively offered in Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad for orders placed prior to 12:00 PM noon.
- **Surcharge**: A fixed premium express surcharge of PKR 450 applies per shipment regardless of cart total.
- **Remote Exclusions**: Next-day delivery cannot be purchased or scheduled for Tier 2 or Tier 3 locations under any circumstances.

## 3. Shipping Charges and Free Delivery Threshold
- **Free Standard Shipping**: Standard surface shipping is completely free on all orders with a total net cart value of PKR 5,000 or greater.
- **Standard Flat Rate**: Orders below PKR 5,000 (such as single replacement filters or fittings) incur a flat nationwide shipping fee of PKR 250.

## 4. Packaging and Fragile Item Handling
Water and air purifiers contain sensitive electronic components, glass touch panels, and RO membranes.
- Units are encased in custom high-density molded EPS foam and double-walled corrugated shipping cartons.
- Every shipment is sealed with tamper-evident security holographic tape. If the tape is severed or carton crushed, customers must refuse delivery and photograph package before signing.

## 5. Failed Delivery Attempts and Re-Routing
- Couriers make up to two (2) delivery attempts. On each attempt, courier sends SMS alert and driver attempts phone contact.
- If both attempts fail due to recipient unavailability or wrong address, the parcel is held at the local regional courier hub for 48 hours.
- If unclaimed within 48 hours, shipment returns to our central warehouse in Lahore. Reshipping requires prepayment of return and re-dispatch fees (PKR 500).

## 6. International Shipping Status
Pearlz Home Systems operates strictly within Pakistan. We do NOT offer direct international shipping, export dispatch, or cross-border customs clearance.

## 7. Order Tracking
Upon warehouse dispatch, customers receive an automated SMS and email containing a direct courier tracking tracking URL and 12-digit consignment number (CN).
