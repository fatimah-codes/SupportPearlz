# Pearlz Home Systems — Comprehensive Troubleshooting Guide
Document ID: TS-GDE-2025-V2
Applicable to: AquaPearl 500 Pro, AquaPearl 300, AeroPearl Mini

## 1. Quick Diagnostic Flowchart and Safety Rules
Before attempting any troubleshooting, always disconnect the electrical power supply and close the cold water supply valve. Never dismantle electronic circuit enclosures or open pressurized water manifolds while power is active.

## 2. Table of Symptoms, Probable Causes, and Action Steps

| Symptom # | Observed Symptom | Probable Cause | Recommended Action / Remedy | Severity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| S-01 | No power / Display completely dark | Loose power cord, tripped GFCI socket, or blown internal thermal fuse. | Check wall outlet with another appliance; firmly seat power plug; press reset button on power adapter. If still dark, contact Pearlz support. | Medium |
| S-02 | Purifier beeps three times and the light flashes red (Audible Code E-03) | Sediment pre-filter cartridge (SKU-FP-01) reaches saturation and differential pressure drop triggers safety sensor. | Replace pre-filter sediment cartridge; press and hold the 'RESET' button on front panel for 5 seconds until chime confirms code clearance. | Low |
| S-03 | Very low water flow rate from dispensing tap (<0.2 L/min) | Clogged pre-filters, fouled RO membrane, or inlet water pressure below 15 PSI. | Inspect feed water valve; verify inlet pressure gauge; replace sediment and carbon filter if older than 6 months. | Medium |
| S-04 | Water tastes bitter, strange, or chemical after filter change | Trapped micro-bubbles, loose carbon dust, or failure to perform initial flushing cycle. | Run first-run flushing procedure (see Installation Guide §4): drain 15 to 20 liters of water (approx 2 full tanks) before drinking. | Low |
| S-05 | Purifier runs continuously and booster pump does not shut off | High-pressure cut-off switch failure, tank air bladder depressurised, or RO membrane micro-leak. | Check if storage tank is filling; verify waste water drain stops when tank is full. If pump runs >3 hours nonstop, shut feed valve and call support. | High |
| S-06 | Water leaking from base of unit | Loose quick-connect collet, ruptured PE tubing, or cracked internal filter bowl. | Immediately shut off main water supply valve and unplug power! Inspect quick-connect push-in fittings. Tighten or re-seat tubing with locking clip. | High |
| S-07 | Display shows TDS reading unusually high (>150 PPM output) | RO membrane exhausted, TDS bypass valve opened by mistake, or sediment bypass. | Verify raw tap water TDS; ensure TDS blend valve is turned clockwise to closed position. If TDS remains >20% of tap water TDS, replace RO membrane (SKU-RO-02). | Medium |
| S-08 | High vibration or rattling noise during operation | Air pocket trapped in booster pump head, unlevel surface, or debris in pump diaphragm. | Ensure unit is seated level on rubber feet; bleed trapped air by cycling dispensing tap open/closed 3 times for 10 seconds. | Medium |
| S-09 | Blinking yellow LED on control panel | Filter life countdown warning (filter remaining life under 10%). | Order genuine replacement filter pack. Normal operation will continue for approx 100 liters before lockout. | Low |
| S-10 | Mobile App will not pair via WiFi | Unit is attempting 5 GHz connection; purifier WiFi module only supports 2.4 GHz 802.11b/g/n networks. | Switch mobile device to 2.4 GHz WiFi band; disable cellular data during pairing; press and hold WiFi button on purifier for 3 seconds until LED blinks rapidly. | Low |
| S-11 | Foul odor (swampy / stale smell) from purified water | Bacterial biofilm in storage tank or exhausted post-carbon mineralizer. | Sanitize storage tank using approved Pearlz descaling solution; replace post-carbon mineralizer cartridge (SKU-MN-04). | Medium |
| S-12 | Water leaking from cabinet / underneath sink | External plumbing adapter loose, drain saddle tubing disconnected, or drain pipe blocked. | Disconnect power and shut inlet valve. Cabinet damage is excluded from product warranty. Inspect waste water line connection to sink drain pipe. | High |
| S-13 | AeroPearl Mini red ring blinking | HEPA filter cartridge (SKU-AP-H1) saturated or dust sensor optical lens obstructed by soot. | Vacuum sensor intake chamber on rear with soft brush; replace HEPA filter and hold night mode button for 7 seconds to reset timer. | Low |
| S-14 | Water dispense button unresponsive | Child-lock active (padlock icon illuminated) or moisture on capacitive touch glass. | Press and hold Child-Lock icon for 3 seconds to disengage lock; wipe glass panel dry with microfiber cloth. | Low |
| S-15 | Excessive reject water discharging from waste pipe | Check-valve failed in open state or auto-shutoff valve (ASV) diaphragm ruptured. | Contact Pearlz authorized technician for auto-shutoff valve replacement. | High |
| S-16 | Error code E-05 audible alarm with solid red light | Internal flood detection sensor in the bottom drip pan has detected liquid accumulation. | CRITICAL: The system has activated emergency solenoid shutoff. Turn off cold supply valve immediately. DO NOT attempt to dry and restart without technician inspection. | Critical |

## 3. Mandatory Escalation Criteria (Do NOT Attempt Self-Fix)
Customers must immediately shut off water supply, disconnect electrical cord, and request professional service for:
1. Internal electrical burning smell or smoke.
2. Error E-05 (Internal leak sensor activation).
3. Booster pump operating with loud grinding metal-on-metal noise.
4. Cracked pressurized filter canisters or ruptured membrane housing.
