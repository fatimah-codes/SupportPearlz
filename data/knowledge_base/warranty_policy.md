# Pearlz Home Systems — Warranty Policy
Document ID: POL-WAR-2025-V3
Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Last Revised: October 15, 2025
Applicable Products: AquaPearl 300, AquaPearl 500 Pro, AeroPearl Mini, AquaPearl 200 (Legacy), AeroPearl Classic (Legacy)

## 1. Warranty Period and Coverage Scope
Pearlz Home Systems (Pvt.) Ltd. warrants each new appliance against defects in materials and manufacturing workmanship under normal residential use.
- **AquaPearl 500 Pro**: 24 months standard warranty from the verifiable invoice date.
- **AquaPearl 300**: 24 months standard warranty from the verifiable invoice date.
- **AeroPearl Mini**: 24 months standard warranty from the verifiable invoice date.
- **Discontinued Models (AquaPearl 200 and AeroPearl Classic)**: 12 months remaining extended warranty for units purchased within promotional clearance windows, subject to valid proof of invoice.

### 1.1 Inception Date Calculation
Warranty duration is strictly computed from the original invoice issuance date issued by Pearlz Home Systems or an authorised dealer, NOT the courier delivery date or the installation date. Customers must retain their official stamped invoice or electronic tax receipt.

## 2. Components Covered
The standard 24-month warranty provides full coverage for functional hardware components:
- Purifier main body structure and moulded chassis
- High-pressure booster pump assembly and electronic transformer
- Internal electronic printed circuit boards (PCBs), power supply unit (PSU), and solenoid valves
- Touch interface control panel and integrated digital TDS monitoring display
- Internal high-purity UV sterilization LED lamp module

Coverage covers all necessary original replacement components and certified technician labour for repair or replacement at Pearlz's discretion.

## 3. Explicit Exclusions and Limitations
### 3.1 Consumables Exclusion
Consumable filtration elements are explicitly excluded from warranty repair or replacement once the package seal is broken. Excluded consumables include:
- Sediment Pre-filter cartridges (SKU-FP-01)
- Activated Carbon block filters (SKU-AC-03)
- Reverse Osmosis (RO) membranes (SKU-RO-02)
- Post-Carbon mineralizer cartridges (SKU-MN-04)
- HEPA filter elements (SKU-AP-H1)

Consumable filters are warranted only against out-of-the-box structural defect or media leakage reported within 48 hours of delivery.

### 3.2 Unauthorised Service, Tampering, and External Damage
Warranty coverage is immediately null and void under any of the following circumstances:
1. **Unauthorised Servicing**: If the appliance casing, internal tubing, or electronic housing is opened, serviced, modified, or repaired by any unauthorised third-party technician, local plumber, or customer without written authorisation from Pearlz Technical Support.
2. **Third-Party Consumables**: The installation of non-genuine, third-party filter cartridges or uncalibrated RO membranes.
3. **Improper Operating Environment**: Operating with feed water exceeding 2,000 PPM TDS, water pressure exceeding 50 PSI without an installed pressure reducer, or direct hot water supply (>45°C).
4. **Physical and Incidental Damage**: Drops, physical impact, electrical surges due to ungrounded sockets, lightning strikes, or insect infestations.
5. **Cabinet and Property Damage**: Damage to kitchen cabinets, countertops, walls, or flooring resulting from water leakages, condensation, or improper self-installation is strictly excluded from coverage; Pearlz is liable only for repair or replacement of the purifier unit itself as set forth in Service Agreement §5.1.

## 4. Transferability on Resale
Warranty coverage is granted strictly to the original registered retail purchaser and is non-transferable upon private resale, second-hand gifting, or transfer of ownership. Any change in registered customer details without Pearlz warranty transfer endorsement voids ongoing claim eligibility.

## 5. Extended Warranty Terms for Discontinued Models
Discontinued product lines (AquaPearl 200 and AeroPearl Classic) remain eligible for paid extended service maintenance. Where an active extended warranty was purchased prior to official product obsolescence, Pearlz guarantees availability of functional mechanical spares or equivalent replacement parts for a period of 36 months from product discontinuation date.

## 6. How to Claim Warranty
To file a warranty claim:
Customers must NOT dispatch the unit to corporate headquarters or retail outlets without a Return Merchandise Authorisation (RMA) number. Claims must be initiated according to the unified Service & Maintenance Agreement §4.2, submitting invoice copy, serial number photo, and fault video to claims@pearlzhome.example.
